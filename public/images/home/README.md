# BAGATI homepage photography

The carousel uses local, optimized photographs as category mood imagery. They are illustrative only and do not imply that BAGATI sells or is affiliated with any pictured brand. Product tiles are populated only from the marketplace catalog.

## Current carousel assets

- `hero-tech.webp` — a warm-neutral, multi-device flat lay featuring a smartphone, smartwatch, wireless earbuds and case, with a separate wireless over-ear headphone photo arranged as a framed inset. Sources: [Pexels photo 2933606](https://www.pexels.com/photo/arranged-mobile-phone-with-gadgets-2933606/) and [Pexels photo 3394650](https://www.pexels.com/photo/white-wireless-headphones-3394650/). Both are available under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/), which permits use in websites and e-commerce shops.
- `hero-fashion.webp` — a centered 3:2 composition showing all three unbranded handbags in gray, caramel, and beige tones, including their handles and outer edges. It uses a crop of the original portrait photo with the photo's own neutral background extended to the sides for the wide hero panel. Source: [Unsplash photo](https://unsplash.com/photos/three-different-types-of-purses-on-a-white-background-eCSEAlw85zc), available under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license).
- `hero-home.webp` — the approved neutral living-room interior, by [Spacejoy](https://unsplash.com/@spacejoy). [Source photo](https://unsplash.com/photos/white-and-brown-living-room-set-9M66C_w_ToM). This asset remains unchanged.

The tech and fashion assets are optimized as local 1800×1200 WebP files. Photographs are illustrative only and do not imply that BAGATI sells or is affiliated with any pictured brand. Product tiles are populated only from the marketplace catalog.
